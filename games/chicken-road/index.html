How to play

  1. Choose a bet and a difficulty, then press Play.
  2. Press Go to hop the chicken to the next manhole. Each safe hop raises the multiplier.
  3. Any manhole can burst into flames. If it does, the round is lost.
  4. Press Cash out after at least one hop to keep the current multiplier.
